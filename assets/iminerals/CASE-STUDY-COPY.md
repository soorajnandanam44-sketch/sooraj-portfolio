# iMinerals

Everyday wellness, clearly expressed.

Brand identity · Packaging · Art direction · Digital design

## Project overview

A visual identity that brings a warm, confident presence to a supplement range. Burgundy glass, expressive typography, and natural imagery connect the packaging to a wider brand experience.

## The problem

The design challenge was to communicate a supplement range with warmth and clarity, while giving each product a recognizable place within one brand.

The visual system needed to make the product name easy to find, give supporting details a clear order, and carry the same personality across packaging and digital touchpoints.

## The solution

A warm identity. A clear system.

- **Recognizable:** Deep burgundy and a prominent wordmark create a consistent visual signature.
- **Readable:** A clear type hierarchy separates the brand, product name, and supporting information.
- **Approachable:** Natural light and tactile surfaces bring warmth to the product presentation.

## Design language

A palette drawn from the artwork pairs deep burgundy with warm ivory, silver, and dark ink. The balance feels considered while keeping the product in focus.

The contrast between an expressive serif and a direct sans serif gives the identity character while keeping product names and supporting details easy to scan.

## Packaging

One system. Distinct products.

The vertical brand name anchors the pack. The product name takes priority, followed by the formula descriptor and capsule count. Consistent placement and contrast give each product its own voice within the same visual family.

## Art direction

Natural light. Tactile surfaces. Product first.

Translucent burgundy glass and a brushed silver lid bring depth and contrast to the packaging presentation. Close framing makes those material choices part of the brand story.

## Digital extension

The same identity, online.

Burgundy typography, product imagery, and nature-led scenes carry the visual language into the homepage.

## Design outcome

From a single pack to a consistent brand.

The colour palette, typography, packaging hierarchy, and imagery work together across the project. The result is a connected visual system for iMinerals, with a clear direction for both print and digital.
