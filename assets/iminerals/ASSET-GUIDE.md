## Editing

The PowerPoint keeps the case-study copy, headings, typography samples, and colour swatches editable. The supplied branding artwork and new product concept images remain embedded images. For consistent sharing, use the PDF or PNG exports. Presentation typography uses Nimbus Roman and Nimbus Sans; another computer may substitute fonts if these are unavailable.
