01 / HERO CONCEPT & ART DIRECTION

01 / THE CHALLENGE

Clarity and recognition across a growing range.

The design challenge was to communicate a supplement range with warmth and clarity, while giving each product a recognizable place within one brand.

The visual system needed to make the product name easy to find, give supporting details a clear order, and carry the same personality across packaging and digital touchpoints.

PROBLEM STATEMENT 02 / 10

02 / THE STRATEGY

A warm identity. A clear system.

Three foundational pillars designed to balance pharmaceutical credibility with lifestyle warmth across physical packaging and interactive touchpoints.

PILLAR 01

Recognizable

Deep burgundy and a prominent wordmark create a consistent, memorable visual signature that stands out on shelf and digital shelves.

PILLAR 02

Readable

A structured typographical hierarchy separates the brand, product name, and supporting information for effortless visual scanning.

PILLAR 03

Approachable

Natural light and tactile surfaces bring warmth and human emotion to the supplement presentation, avoiding clinical coldness.

STRATEGY & ARCHITECTURE 03 / 10

03 / VISUAL LANGUAGE

Considered balance & expressive contrast.

A palette drawn from the artwork pairs deep burgundy with warm ivory, silver, and dark ink. The balance feels considered while keeping the product in focus. The contrast between an expressive serif and a direct sans serif gives the identity character while keeping product names and supporting details easy to scan.

04 / PACKAGING SYSTEM

One system. Distinct products.

The vertical brand name anchors the pack. The product name takes priority, followed by the formula descriptor and capsule count. Consistent placement and contrast give each product its own voice within the same visual family.

05 / ART DIRECTION

Natural light. Tactile surfaces. Product first.

Translucent burgundy glass and a brushed silver lid bring depth and contrast to the packaging presentation. Close framing makes those material choices part of the brand story.
