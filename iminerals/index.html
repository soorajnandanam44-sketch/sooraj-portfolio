01 / THE PROJECT

for iMinerals.

02 / THE PROBLEM

Clarity without
losing character.

The design challenge was to communicate a supplement range with warmth and clarity, while giving each product a recognizable place within one brand.

The visual system needed to make the product name easy to find, give supporting details a clear order, and carry the same personality across packaging and digital touchpoints.

03 / THE SOLUTION

A warm identity.
A clear system.

01

Recognizable

Deep burgundy and a prominent wordmark create a consistent visual signature.

02

Readable

A clear type hierarchy separates the brand, product name, and supporting information.

03

Approachable

Natural light and tactile surfaces bring warmth to the product presentation.

05 / PACKAGING

One system.
Distinct products.

The vertical brand name anchors the pack. The product name takes priority, followed by the formula descriptor and capsule count.

Consistent placement and contrast give each product its own voice within the same visual family.
